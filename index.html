How the statistical forecast works

For each product, use up to 28 records strictly before the forecast date. The latest 7 sales receive increasing weights (1 through 7). Combine 65% of this weighted moving average with 35% of the average sales on the matching weekday. Without matching weekdays, use the weighted average alone. With fewer than 3 records, show a low-data estimate; without records, request a manual plan.

Weather, holiday and event percentages are configurable assumptions, not effects learned from history. Their percentages are added together, with a minimum total multiplier of 0.1. Weather labels alone do not change demand. Sell-outs are flagged because recorded sales may understate demand. These estimates cannot guarantee accuracy or waste reduction. Saved forecasts retain their original values for later comparison; recalculation replaces the same date’s forecast.

Your data, in your hands

Records are saved only in localStorage in this browser and device. Clearing browser data removes them. Export CSV regularly for backup. Sales CSV includes product details; full JSON backup also preserves forecasts and plans.

CSV imports validate the entire file before saving. Duplicate product/date rows update existing records. Unknown products are created from CSV price, cost and batch fields. Exported rows retain the price and cost at the time of entry.
